About AsianHOST 2026

The 11th Asian Hardware Oriented Security and Trust Symposium (AsianHOST 2026) brings together researchers, students and practitioners working on hardware and system security, trust, secure architectures, circuits, design methods and related technologies.

AsianHOST supports the growth of hardware security research and collaboration across Asia and the Pacific region. The 2026 edition will take place from 11–13 December 2026 at IIT Kharagpur Research Park, Rajarhat, Kolkata, India.

The conference is hosted by the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur. For the technical programme, committee information and conference announcements, please visit the official AsianHOST 2026 website.
